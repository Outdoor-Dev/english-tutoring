Hi, I’m Monica.

My favorite part of teaching English is the moment someone goes, “Wait, that’s what they’re asking?” Works for homework directions and emails from your boss.

I grew up in Miami and spent ten years living in Colombia. Since 2023, I’ve taught English to children and adults in South Korea, from beginners building their first sentences to advanced learners who want to sound more natural at work and in everyday conversations.

I tutor U.S. K–12 students through Air Tutors. I’ve also homeschooled my own children across all the core subjects throughout their elementary years. When a student gets stuck, I like to help them find one thing they can try right away. That might mean putting an assignment into their own words or trying a new phrase in a conversation.
